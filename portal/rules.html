Portal Rules & Regulations
Account Security
  • Keep your credentials safe: Never share your User ID, email, phone, or password with anyone.
  • Password requirements: Use strong passwords with at least 8 characters, including letters and numbers.
  • Regular updates: Change your password periodically and whenever you suspect unauthorized access.
  • Session management: Always log out after using the portal, especially on shared devices.
  • Phishing awareness: Darul Aman Academy will never ask for your password via email or phone.
Student Responsibilities
  • Academic integrity: Maintain honesty in all academic activities and assessments.
  • Attendance: Regular attendance is mandatory. Report absences with valid reasons.
  • Assignment submission: Submit all assignments on time through the portal.
  • Respectful communication: Use appropriate language when communicating with teachers and staff.
  • Hifz progress: Hifz students must maintain daily practice and report progress honestly.
Guardian Responsibilities
  • Monitor progress: Regularly check your child's academic and attendance reports.
  • Fee payment: Pay all fees on time as per the academy's fee schedule.
  • Communication: Maintain regular contact with teachers and administration regarding your child's progress.
  • Authorized access: Only guardians with proper authorization can access student information.
  • Support learning: Ensure your child has adequate time and environment for studies at home.
Teacher Responsibilities
  • Accurate records: Maintain accurate attendance, grades, and progress records.
  • Timely feedback: Provide prompt feedback on assignments and assessments.
  • Professional conduct: Maintain professional relationships with students and guardians.
  • Curriculum delivery: Follow the approved curriculum and teaching standards.
  • Hifz evaluation: Hifz teachers must conduct regular and fair evaluations of student progress.
Portal Usage Guidelines
  • Authorized use only: Use the portal only for legitimate academy-related activities.
  • Data privacy: Respect the privacy of other students, guardians, and staff members.
  • No unauthorized access: Do not attempt to access other users' accounts or data.
  • Report issues: Immediately report any technical issues or security concerns to administration.
  • Acceptable use: Do not use the portal for commercial, political, or inappropriate purposes.
Disciplinary Actions
  • Policy violations: Violation of these rules may result in account suspension or termination.
  • Academic misconduct: Cheating, plagiarism, or academic dishonesty will be dealt with severely.
  • Behavioral issues: Inappropriate behavior towards staff or other students may lead to disciplinary action.
  • Appeal process: Users have the right to appeal disciplinary decisions through proper channels.
  • Legal compliance: Serious violations may be referred to appropriate authorities.
Contact & Support
  • Technical support: For portal issues, contact the IT department or administration.
  • Academic concerns: For academic matters, contact the respective teachers or department heads.
  • Administration: For general inquiries, contact the academy administration during office hours.
  • Emergency: For urgent matters, use the provided emergency contact numbers.
